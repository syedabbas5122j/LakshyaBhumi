# Landing Page Style Observations

## Reference sources

- User-provided screenshot: a warm, hand-drawn civic/land-information landing page concept with a top navigation, central headline, map illustration, metric cards, an About section, and a simple footer.
- Canva reference: `https://canva.link/ytbuvjhcy4k9bi5`

## Canva reference style

The accessible Canva design is a minimal, editorial hospitality website. Its main visual traits are:

- Large photographic hero composition with a strong brand name and a short location/category label.
- A restrained, warm neutral palette based around light grey, dark greybrown, white, and muted black.
- Generous whitespace and wide horizontal sections.
- Serif display typography paired with clean sans-serif supporting text.
- Very short navigation and direct calls to action such as “Book your stay”.
- Repeated image-led content sections with simple labels and concise descriptions.
- Thin dividers, small uppercase labels, and quiet hierarchy rather than dense UI.

## Translation into BhoomiSync

The landing page keeps that calm editorial rhythm but changes the subject and palette for SIH 26013:

- `rgb(248, 186, 0)` is the signal/accent color for calls to action, confidence, and highlights.
- `rgb(51, 134, 55)` is used for land, validation, and trusted outcomes.
- `rgb(49, 152, 146)` is used for water, spatial intelligence, links, and active states.
- Warm paper and cream backgrounds retain the reference's quiet premium feel.
- Space Grotesk supplies the geometric display voice, DM Sans keeps body copy readable, and a restrained Georgia italic adds an editorial land-record feel.
- The hero replaces a hotel photograph with a CSS-built parcel map so the product is visible immediately and no external image dependency is required.
- The page uses a map-led hero, three capability cards, a workflow diagram, a field-inspired About section, and a focused contact band.

## Interaction notes

- Navigation collapses into a mobile menu.
- Scroll reveals gently animate sections into view.
- Platform statistics count up when they enter the viewport.
- All page links scroll to working sections; the contact action opens an email draft.
